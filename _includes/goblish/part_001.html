Quick grammar

Productive endings

-ik plural • -ta past • -um ongoing • -esh possessive • -li small/dear • -gor great/intense • -ar person who acts • -sha manner

Clause pattern

subject – verb – object; noun – adjective; na before a negative; ra at the end of a question

Respect

Use hardship titles, gifts, fulfilled promises, and repetition. Izka-durr means Fire-Survivor. gobdurr and gobdurrgor are hardship-evolved Goblin forms. Repeating a title twice is formal respect; three times is solemn.
